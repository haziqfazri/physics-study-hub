Data Collection Steps ​

  1. Record the falling slotted mass, m
  2. Choose a fixed point at a height, h above the floor and record this height
  3. Wind the string around the axle
  4. Release the slotted mass, m from the fixed height, h
  5. Record the time, t for the slotted mass to reach the floor
  6. Repeat steps 4-8 for at least six different slotted masses, m
