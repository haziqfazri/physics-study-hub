Apparatus ​

  • G-clamp
  • Solenoid (about 100 turns) or ticker timer
  • AC power supply (2–4 V)
  • Metal rod (soft iron)
  • Two bar magnets
  • Magnet holder
  • Piece of string (approximately 2 m long)
  • Pulley with clamp
  • Wooden wedges
  • Set of slotted masses: 2 g, 5 g, 10 g, and 20 g
  • Metre rule
  • Connecting wires
  • Wooden block

Procedure ​

  1. Set up the apparatus as shown in Figure 6.1

  1. Connect the terminals of the solenoid to the AC power supply (2 V, 50 Hz)

    Caution: Do not exceed 4 V to avoid damage to the solenoid

  2. Place the metal rod between the two bar magnets

  3. Tie one end of the string to the rod and the other to the hook of the slotted mass

    Important: Make sure that the length of the string from the end of the rod to the pulley is not less than 1.5 m

  4. Clamp the metal rod properly

  5. Switch on the power supply

  6. Adjust the position of the metal rod to get maximum vibration

  7. Place the wooden wedges below the string as close as possible to the pulley

  8. Adjust the position of the wooden wedges until a clear single loop standing wave (fundamental mode) is observed

  9. Record the distance, l between the wedges (this is the vibrating length)
