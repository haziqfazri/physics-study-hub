Procedure ​

A. Free Fall Motion ​

  1. Set up the apparatus as shown in Figure 2.1.

  1. Switch on the circuit and attach the steel ball onto the upper contact.

  2. Adjust the height h of the electromagnet above the point of impact.

  3. Switch off the circuit and let the ball fall. Record the height, h and time, t.

  4. Repeat steps (3) and (4) for at least six different heights, h.

  5. Tabulate the data in a table.

  6. Plot a graph of h against t².

  7. Determine the acceleration due to gravity g from the gradient of the graph using:

  8. Determine the uncertainty of acceleration due to gravity Δg.

B. Projectile Motion ​

  1. Set up the apparatus as shown in Figure 2.2.

  1. Release the steel ball on the curvature railing at least six different heights, h and record the range, R.

  2. Tabulate the data in a table.

  3. Plot a graph of h against R².

  4. Measure the height of the table H (the edge of the railing to the landing surface)

  5. By referring to the graph of h against t² from Experiment A, obtain the value of t² for H using extrapolation.

  6. Determine the acceleration due to gravity g from the gradient of the h vs R² graph.

  7. Determine the uncertainty of acceleration due to gravity Δg.

  8. Compare the acceleration due to gravity g obtained from both experiments with the standard value (≈ 9.81 m/s²).
