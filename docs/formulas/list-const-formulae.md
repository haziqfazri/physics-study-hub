| Acceleration in SHM | $a = -\omega^2 A\sin(\omega t) = -\omega^2 y$ |
| Kinetic Energy in SHM| $K = \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2 (A^2 - y^2)$ |

</div>

### Topic 9 - Kinetic Theory of Gases and Thermodynamics

<div class='center-table'>

| Formula Name | Equation |
|:------------:|:---------:|

| | $v_{rms} = \sqrt{\frac{3kT}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{3RT}{M}}$ |

| Isochoric Process | $W = \int P dV = 0$ |
