# Lab Report Writing Format

## 1. Title 

- State the title of the experiment.
> *e.g.: Experiment 2: Free Fall and Projectile Motion*

## 2. Objective

- State clearly all objectives contained inside of the lab manual
> *e.g.: To verify the conservation of angular momentum*

## 3. Theory

- Copy the theory from the lab manual including the equations

## 4. Apparatus

- List all apparatus in the lab manual
- State name, range, and sensitivity 
> *e.g.: Thermometer: −10 °C to 110 °C, sensitivity: ± 1 °C*

## 5. Procedure

- Write in passive form about all the steps taken during the experiment. Diagram must be included

## 6. Observation

Must contain all the following:

### i. Data Tabulation
- Table **contains symbol and unit of quantities** (including uncertainty of apparatus)
- **Consistent decimal places** of measured data follow the uncertainty of the measuring apparatus 
- Extra column for graph plotting or calculation *(only when necessary)*

### ii. Graph
- Title
- Axis label with unit
- Suitable scale (no break scale if possible)
- Graph size is > 60% of the graph paper
- All points plotted correctly
- Centroid point plotted correctly
- Best fit line passes through centroid and balanced
- Triangle gradient is drawn with dashed line and its size ≥ half the size of the graph line

### iii. Calculation
- All final answers must be in **2 decimal places / 3 significant figures** *(where applicable)*
- Calculation of uncertainties using LSM method and basic combination of uncertainties
- All final answers must be written with correct unit *(where applicable)*

## 7. Discussion

### i. Percentage Difference
Show the calculation of the percentage difference of experimental and theoretical values.

$$\% \text{ difference} = \frac{|X_{theory} - X_{experiment}|}{X_{theory}} \times 100\%$$

### ii. Comment on Experimental Value
Compare with the theoretical value and state whether the experiment is successful or not (< 10% = success).

> *e.g.: "In this experiment, the specific latent heat of fusion of ice obtained is 363 J g⁻¹ is greater than the theoretical value which is 340 J g⁻¹. However, the experiment can be considered successful because the percentage difference obtained is less than 10%."*

### iii. Precaution Steps (at least 1)
Actions that can be taken by students to minimise effect on the result of experiment.

> *e.g.: "Avoid parallax error by placing the eye perpendicular to the scale of the thermometer."*

### iv. Sources of Error (at least 2)
Limits of either instrument or procedure that prevent more accurate measurements of data.

> *e.g.: "The ice and water mixture are not stirred constantly, causing the final temperature to be too warm."*

> **Reminder:** Do not only state the sources of error as random and systematic errors.

### v. Suggestions to Overcome Error (at least 2)
Actions of improvement that can be made to either the instrument or procedure to increase measurement accuracy.

> *e.g.: "The ice and water mixture must be stirred constantly to ensure that the temperature throughout the water is uniform."*

### vi. Answer Manual Questions
Answer all questions given in the manual procedure (if any).

## 8. Conclusion

State briefly the experimental result with reference to the objectives of the experiment.

Write the experimental value obtained and its uncertainty with correct unit, or any other required conclusion based on the objective of the experiment given.

> *e.g.: "The relationship between voltage and current was investigated to determine the resistance of a constantan wire. The resistance was found to be $(5.20 \pm 0.10) \Omega$. Since the graph V against I yielded a straight line passing through the origin, Ohm's Law is verified for this conductor.*
